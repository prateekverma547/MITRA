# AI Engineer, Applied LLM

*A sample job description for trying MITRA. The company and role are invented.*

**Business unit:** AI
**Location:** Noida, hybrid
**Experience:** 3 to 6 years

## About the role

You will build and run the language-model features our clients use every day:
retrieval over their documents, structured extraction, and voice assistants.
You will own a feature from the first prototype to the service in production,
and you will be the person a client asks why the model behaved as it did.

## What you will do

- Design retrieval and extraction pipelines over client documents.
- Build evaluation sets with the client, and report results honestly,
  including where the model fails.
- Ship services that stay up: monitoring, alerts, rollbacks.
- Explain trade-offs to product managers and clients who are not engineers.

## What we are looking for

- Three or more years building machine learning or LLM systems that real users
  rely on.
- Hands-on evaluation work: you have built a test set and defended a number.
- Python, an API framework such as FastAPI, and one cloud platform.
- Clear written and spoken English.

## Nice to have

- Speech-to-text or voice agents.
- Fine-tuning a small model for a narrow task.
