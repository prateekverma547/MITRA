# Social Media Content Specialist

**Location:** Noida (hybrid, 3 days in office)
**Experience:** 2 to 5 years
**Team:** Marketing

## About the role

You will run our social channels day to day and make most of what goes on them.
We are a services company, so the audience is other businesses, and the job is to
sound like people who build software rather than like a brand account.

You will write, brief design, edit video, schedule, and answer what comes back.

## What you will do

- Own the content calendar across LinkedIn, X, Instagram and YouTube, and decide
  what each channel is for
- Write posts and short scripts, and brief the designer or editor with enough
  detail that the first version is close
- Turn one piece of long form work, such as a case study, into a month of posts
  without it reading as the same thing four times
- Handle comments and inbound messages, including the ones that are complaints
- Report on what performed, with a view on why, and change the plan because of it
- Work with recruiters on employer brand content, which is a real part of this

## What we are looking for

- 2 or more years running social for a company or a client, not only personal
  channels
- Writing you can show us, and a clear sense of what does not work
- Basic video editing, enough to cut a talking head clip and caption it
- Judgement about tone in public, especially when something goes wrong
- Some grasp of what a post is meant to achieve beyond engagement

## Nice to have

- Experience with a B2B or technical audience
- Design tools such as Figma or Canva to a working level
- Paid social experience, even at a small budget
