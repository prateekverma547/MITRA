# Marketing Manager

**Location:** Noida (hybrid, 3 days in office)
**Experience:** 4 to 8 years
**Team:** Marketing

## About the role

You will own demand generation for a services business. That means the pipeline
is the point: not impressions, not follower counts, but qualified conversations
with companies who are deciding whether to hire an engineering partner.

You will work with sales on what a good lead looks like, and you will be held to
whether the leads you generate turn into calls that go somewhere.

## What you will do

- Own the marketing plan and the budget against it, and be able to say what each
  line is expected to return
- Run paid campaigns across search and LinkedIn, and decide when to stop one
- Work with the content team on what gets written, chosen against what buyers
  actually search for rather than what we find interesting
- Build the reporting that connects spend to pipeline, and present it monthly to
  people who will challenge it
- Run the website as a conversion surface, including the testing programme
- Brief and manage external agencies and freelancers where we use them

## What we are looking for

- 4 or more years in B2B marketing, ideally selling a service rather than a
  product with a self serve signup
- A campaign you can talk through end to end: the thesis, the spend, the result,
  and what you would do differently
- Fluency with attribution and its limits, and honesty about what you cannot
  measure
- Experience working alongside a sales team, including the disagreements
- Comfort with a long sales cycle, where this quarter's spend shows up later

## Nice to have

- Experience marketing to a technical buyer, such as a CTO or a head of product
- HubSpot or a comparable CRM and automation stack
- Account based marketing on a named target list
