# Mobile Engineer (React Native)

**Location:** Noida (hybrid, 3 days in office)
**Experience:** 3 to 7 years
**Team:** Mobile Engineering

## About the role

You will build mobile applications for clients, mostly in React Native, and you
will own what you ship through to the store. Our clients are in health, retail,
hospitality and fintech, and they generally want something in the stores on a
date rather than an architecture they can admire.

This is hands on. You will write code most days and you will be the person a
client's technical lead talks to about why the app behaves the way it does.

## What you will do

- Build and ship React Native applications to both stores, including the release
  itself rather than handing it to somebody else
- Write native modules when the bridge is the wrong answer, in Swift or Kotlin
- Own performance on real devices, including the cheap Android handsets a lot of
  our users actually have
- Handle the parts that break in the field: offline state, flaky networks, push,
  deep links, permissions
- Work with the API team on contracts, and push back on a payload that will cost
  the client's battery
- Take part in code review, and be able to explain a change to somebody junior

## What we are looking for

- 3 or more years shipping mobile applications that real people installed
- React Native to depth, including where it leaks into native
- An app you can talk through: what was hard, what you got wrong, what you would
  build differently
- Experience with store review, including a rejection you had to resolve
- Enough native knowledge to debug a crash that the JavaScript layer cannot see

## Nice to have

- Native Android or iOS work in its own right
- CI for mobile, such as Fastlane or a comparable pipeline
- Experience with a design system across several client projects
