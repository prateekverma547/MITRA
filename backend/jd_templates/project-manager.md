# Project Manager

**Location:** Noida (hybrid, 3 days in office)
**Experience:** 5 to 9 years
**Team:** Delivery

## About the role

You will run client engagements end to end: the plan, the team, the budget, and
the relationship. In a services business the project manager is the person the
client calls when something has gone wrong, and the person the team relies on to
have said something before it did.

You will usually be running two or three engagements at once, at different stages.

## What you will do

- Own the plan and the estimate, and keep both honest as the work changes
- Run the delivery rhythm: standups, sprint planning, demos, retrospectives
- Manage scope against a signed statement of work, and raise a change request
  rather than absorbing it quietly
- Report status to the client, including the weeks where the news is bad
- Manage the commercials on the engagement: burn against budget, invoicing
  milestones, and the margin
- Resolve the resourcing conflicts that come with a shared team
- Run risk properly, which means saying what could go wrong while it is still
  cheap to act on

## What we are looking for

- 5 or more years running software delivery, agency or consultancy preferred
- A project that went wrong that you can talk about clearly, including your part
  in it
- Comfort telling a client something they do not want to hear, early
- Working understanding of how the software gets built, enough to challenge an
  estimate without pretending to be an engineer
- Experience with fixed price or milestone based work, not only time and materials

## Nice to have

- Formal certification such as PMP or a scrum credential, though we care more
  about the projects
- Experience managing a distributed team across time zones
- Pre sales exposure, shaping an engagement before it is won
