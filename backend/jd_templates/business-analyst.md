# Business Analyst

**Location:** Noida (hybrid, 3 days in office)
**Experience:** 3 to 6 years
**Team:** Delivery

## About the role

You will sit between a client and the engineering team that builds for them. Most
of our engagements start with a client who knows the outcome they want and has
not yet decided what the software should do, so the first job is turning that
into something a team can estimate and build.

This is a client facing role. You will run the workshops, write the requirements,
and be the person who says out loud when a late change is going to cost the date.

## What you will do

- Run discovery with client stakeholders and turn what you hear into a BRD or a
  set of user stories the team can actually estimate
- Write acceptance criteria that a tester can work from without asking you what
  you meant
- Keep a change log of scope decisions, and make sure the client has agreed to
  each one in writing before it reaches the sprint
- Hold UAT with the client, triage what comes out of it, and separate a defect
  from a change request without letting it become an argument
- Work with the project manager on estimates, and be able to defend the numbers
- Model the current and proposed process, usually in BPMN or plain flowcharts

## What we are looking for

- 3 or more years as a business analyst on software delivery, agency or product
- Requirements writing you can show us, and a view on what makes a bad one
- Comfort running a room where the client's stakeholders disagree with each other
- Enough technical grounding to know when an ask is expensive, and to ask the
  engineers the right question before promising anything
- Experience with UAT and sign off on a project that shipped

## Nice to have

- Domain experience in one of health, retail, hospitality or fintech
- Jira and Confluence, or a comparable stack
- Exposure to fixed price engagements, where scope control is the whole job
